XII. Compressed Master View


The entire Universal Primitive Graph can be stated in one compressed chain:


Entities bounded into systems enter relations.
Relations under constraints generate states and processes.
State-process systems produce trajectories, flows, and dependencies.
Flows carrying information enable memory, models, and regulation.
Regulated systems under selection and emergence yield adaptive complexity.
Adaptive complexity capable of modeling itself yields observers.
Observers externalizing models through symbols yield narrative and culture.
Culture recursively modeling its own models yields reflexive knowledge systems.


That is the most compressed exact statement of the framework so far.
