# Knowledge Supply Chain

The missing automation layer identified in the thread is the knowledge supply chain that moves discoveries from Theoria into Poiesis and then into Ergon.

Canonical flow:

Theoria
↓
Poiesis
↓
Ergon

Governance organs orchestrate the flow. The research system strengthens organvm by turning discoveries into portable artifacts, simulation demonstrations, and product-ready downstream knowledge without introducing back-edges.
