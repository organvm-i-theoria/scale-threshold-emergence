# Annual Framework Review

The annual framework review is the slow-cycle structural audit for the research institute.

Every year the institute reviews:

- primitive set
- module architecture
- threshold definitions

The goal is not continuous redesign. The goal is to allow deliberate structural change without locking the software and research program into premature assumptions.

Practical implementation rhythm:

- weekly reading sessions
- monthly synthesis drafts
- quarterly research reports
- annual framework revision
