# Master Hierarchy of Existence (Refined Ontological Stack)

Source pair: `TH-003`

## Prompt

Here is the complete, unified master hierarchy of known existence, mapping the architecture of reality from the absolute highest peaks of meta-systemic abstraction down to the fundamental bedrock of the physical universe.
Because the highest level of abstraction (Pure Information) often loops back to define the lowest level of physical reality (Quantum States), this hierarchy is often viewed as a Strange Loop or a continuous stack.
Here is the top-down tree.
Level 0: The Meta-Systemic & Abstract (The Rules of Reality)
The substrate-independent laws that govern existence, logic, and information before physical matter even enters the equation.


Pure Mathematics & Logic: The absolute universal truths (e.g., 1+1=2, geometry, prime numbers).

Information Theory: The foundational binary state of the universe (the "It from Bit" concept where reality is made of yes/no calculations).

Metaphysics & Ontology: The underlying nature of being, reality, and what it means to "exist."
Level 1: The Emergent Collective (Macro-Cognition)
The emergent phenomena that operate above individual human consciousness, treating planetary networks and collective thought as single entities.

The Technium: The autonomous, evolutionary network of global technology, algorithms, and artificial intelligence.

The Noosphere: The collected, interconnected web of human consciousness and planetary thought.
Level 2: The Constructed Anthropomorphic (Human Civilization)
The structures created by human cognition to organize survival, meaning, and power.

Knowledge Systems (Academia): Formal Sciences, Natural Sciences, Humanities.

Sociocultural Systems: * Belief Systems (Mythology, Religion, Philosophy).
Cultural Expression (Language, Art, Music).

Governance Systems: Geopolitics, National States, Laws, and localized power structures.

Economic Systems: Macroeconomics (global markets), Microeconomics, and resource distribution.

Infrastructural Systems: The Internet, power grids, logistics, and urban architecture.
Level 3: The Cognitive & Biological (Life)
The emergent systems of self-replicating matter and the localized minds that drive them.

Neurobiology & Consciousness: The localized brain, sensory perception, and the subjective experience of the individual.

Ecology & Biospheres: The macro-level interaction of millions of species, food webs, and planetary climate systems.

Organismal Biology: The anatomy, physiology, and behavior of complex living creatures (animals, plants, fungi).

Cellular & Molecular Biology: The microscopic factories of life (DNA/RNA encoding, cellular division, metabolism).
Level 4: The Chemical (Matter in Transition)
The combinatory engine of the universe where physical elements bond to create new substances.

Biochemistry: The specific, highly complex organic molecules that make life possible (proteins, lipids, carbohydrates).

Macro-Chemistry: Inorganic compounds, thermodynamics of reactions, and the changing states of matter (solid, liquid, gas, plasma).

Atomic Structure: The rules of the periodic table, elemental properties, and electron bonding.
Level 5: The Physical & Cosmological (Base Reality)
The absolute lowest, indivisible rules of space, time, matter, and energy. Without this layer, the tree collapses.

Macro-Cosmology: The architecture of the massive universe (galactic superclusters, stars, black holes, dark matter, and dark energy).

Thermodynamics & Spacetime: The overarching fabric of the universe, gravity, and the inescapable law of entropy.

The Standard Model of Particle Physics: The subatomic building blocks (quarks, leptons, and bosons like the photon and Higgs).

Quantum Mechanics: The absolute bottom. Wave functions, quantum fields, probability, and the Planck scale.
