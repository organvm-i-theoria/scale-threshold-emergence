VIII. Minimal Universal Grammar v1


Based on the recurrence map, the project’s first-pass universal grammar can already be stated:


Entities enter relations.
Relations under constraints produce systems.
Systems occupy states.
States change through processes.
Processes generate dependencies and flows.
Flows carry information.
Information supports modeling.
Models enable prediction.
Prediction plus feedback stabilizes adaptation.
Adaptation across networks yields emergence.
Emergence under symbolic mediation yields culture, narrative, and higher-order civilization.


That is the first reasonably compact statement of the full-stack architecture.
