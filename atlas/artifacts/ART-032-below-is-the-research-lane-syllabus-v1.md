## Response

Below is the Research Lane Syllabus v1.
This converts the Canonical Source Index into a sequenced research program aligned with the threshold transitions of the framework.


Each lane contains 10–12 works, ordered to move from foundational theory → mechanism proposals → synthesis.


The lanes correspond to the five Phase-2 threshold packages:


TP-05  chemistry → life
TP-06  life → cognition
TP-07  cognition → society
TP-08  society → symbolic systems
TP-09  symbolic systems → reflexive knowledge



Lane R2-01


Origin of Life (Chemistry → Biology)


Central question:


How do chemical reaction systems become self-maintaining living systems?


Stage 1 — Foundations


Harold Morowitz — Energy Flow in Biology
Christian de Duve — Vital Dust
Addy Pross — What Is Life?


These establish the thermodynamic and chemical framing.


Stage 2 — Autocatalysis and Complexity


Stuart Kauffman — Origins of Order
Eigen & Schuster — Hypercycle papers
Manfred Eigen — Steps Toward Life


Focus: autocatalytic networks.


Stage 3 — Metabolism and Energy


Nick Lane — The Vital Question
Nick Lane — Transformer


Focus: energy gradients and metabolic origin.


Stage 4 — Protocells and Experimental Models


Jack Szostak — protocell research papers
Pier Luigi Luisi — The Emergence of Life


Stage 5 — Systems Synthesis


Smith & Morowitz — The Origin and Nature of Life on Earth



Lane R2-02


Origin of Cognition (Life → Mind)


Central question:


How do adaptive organisms become world-modeling agents?


Stage 1 — Biological Foundations


Antonio Damasio — Self Comes to Mind
Michael Gazzaniga — The Consciousness Instinct


Stage 2 — Philosophical and Cognitive Models


Daniel Dennett — Consciousness Explained
Daniel Dennett — From Bacteria to Bach and Back


Stage 3 — Embodied and Enactive Cognition


Evan Thompson — Mind in Life
Francisco Varela — The Embodied Mind


Stage 4 — Predictive Processing


Andy Clark — Surfing Uncertainty
Karl Friston — predictive processing papers


Stage 5 — Information and Mind


Terrence Deacon — Incomplete Nature



Lane R2-03


Origin of Society (Mind → Social Systems)


Central question:


How do intelligent agents form stable supra-individual systems?


Stage 1 — Cooperation and Game Theory


Robert Axelrod — The Evolution of Cooperation
Mancur Olson — The Logic of Collective Action


Stage 2 — Institutional Theory


Douglass North — Institutions, Institutional Change
Elinor Ostrom — Governing the Commons


Stage 3 — Cultural Evolution


Boyd & Richerson — Culture and the Evolutionary Process
Joseph Henrich — The Secret of Our Success


Stage 4 — Collective Intelligence


Scott Page — Diversity and Complexity
Herbert Simon — bounded rationality papers



Lane R2-04


Origin of Symbolic Systems (Society → Language / Meaning)


Central question:


How do social systems generate stable symbolic meaning systems?


Stage 1 — Semiotic Foundations


Ferdinand de Saussure — Course in General Linguistics
Charles Sanders Peirce — Collected Papers


Stage 2 — Symbolic Evolution


Terrence Deacon — The Symbolic Species


Stage 3 — Cognitive Linguistics


George Lakoff & Mark Johnson — Metaphors We Live By


Stage 4 — Semiotic Theory


Umberto Eco — A Theory of Semiotics


Stage 5 — Myth and Narrative


Joseph Campbell — Hero With a Thousand Faces
Claude Lévi-Strauss — Structural Anthropology



Lane R2-05


Origin of Reflexive Knowledge (Symbolic Systems → Science)


Central question:


How do symbolic cultures develop systems that correct their own knowledge?


Stage 1 — Scientific Method Foundations


Karl Popper — Logic of Scientific Discovery


Stage 2 — Scientific Paradigms


Thomas Kuhn — Structure of Scientific Revolutions


Stage 3 — Methodological Critique


Imre Lakatos — Methodology of Scientific Research Programmes

Stage 4 — Epistemology

Stage 5 — Sociology of Science


Robert Merton — sociology of science papers
Helen Longino — Science as Social Knowledge
Bruno Latour — Science in Action
