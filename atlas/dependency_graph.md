# Dependency Graph

The organism uses a directed dependency graph with strict flow constraints.

Primary production flow:

- Theory (I) → Art (II) → Commerce (III)

Governance organs operate laterally and coordinate orchestration, publication, community, distribution, and meta-mapping without introducing circular dependencies.

Architectural law:

- No back-edges exist in the dependency graph.
- Commerce cannot reach back into Art for runtime dependencies.
- Art cannot reach back into Theory.
