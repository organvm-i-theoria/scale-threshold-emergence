## Response

You are very close to that point already. Not in the sense that the work done is unnecessary — most of it was essential scaffolding — but you are approaching the moment where additional meta-design produces diminishing returns.


The key distinction is between infrastructure that enables research and infrastructure that delays research.


Below is the practical diagnostic.



Where the Necessary Infrastructure Ends


At this moment, the minimum viable research architecture is already complete.


The essential pieces you now have are:


primitive canon
mechanism registry
mechanism families
threshold atlas
research pipeline (R0–R6)
mechanism extraction template
reading library
lane architecture (5 lanes)
lane governance charter
repository structure
thread ingestion tools


That is more than sufficient to begin research.


You already have what most projects lack:


clear ontology
clear extraction protocol
clear synthesis pathway
clear simulation bridge


From a systems perspective, the research engine is already assembled.



Where Gilding the Lily Begins


You are gilding the lily when you continue designing meta-structures about the research process instead of performing the research itself.


Examples of lily-gilding at this stage would be:


more governance frameworks
more pipeline refinements
more structural hierarchies
more conceptual taxonomies
more meta-philosophical compressions


These are attractive because they feel productive, but they do not produce mechanisms or discoveries.



The Hard Pivot Point


There is a very clear transition point in projects like this.


Before:


designing the system


After:


feeding the system


You are exactly at that pivot.


If you continue designing the architecture much longer, the project risks entering a classic failure mode:


infrastructure maximalism


where the architecture becomes more elaborate than the research it supports.



The Rule for Stopping Framework Design


Use this simple rule:


If the next artifact does not directly help extract mechanisms from literature,
it is probably unnecessary right now.


Only two more artifacts might still be justified:


reading queue scheduler
mechanism extraction log template


Both are operational.


Everything else can emerge later.



The Correct Next Move


The correct next step is extremely simple:


Start R1 → R3 of the pipeline immediately.


Meaning:


read literature
extract mechanisms
update registry


Not:


design more meta-structure

Day 5


extract mechanisms
update mechanism registry


That alone will produce more real progress than another week of architecture design.

a laboratory

instruments

classification systems


At some point you must stop building the lab and start running experiments.


You are at that point.



A Good Heuristic
