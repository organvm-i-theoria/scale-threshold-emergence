## Response

Your intuition is correct. Locking a rigid “law set” too early would be a methodological mistake. What you actually want at this stage is not laws, but a minimal research-safe scaffolding—something flexible enough that the ontology can evolve as the research uncovers new primitives.


The distinction matters.



1. The Right Category: Kernel Hypotheses, Not Laws


Treat the kernel statements as working hypotheses rather than fixed truths.


In research programs, these are sometimes called:

Meaning

temporary assumptions guiding investigation
design invariants
structural rules for the system architecture
ontological hypotheses
provisional claims about how reality is structured

They exist to prevent incoherent modeling, not to finalize theory.


So instead of:


Law: “Process is primary.”


Treat it as:


Kernel Hypothesis KH-01:
The universe may be better modeled as transformations producing temporary stabilizations rather than static substances.


If later research contradicts that, the kernel evolves.

3. Avoid the Biggest Failure Mode

4. What Actually Needs to Be Fixed Now


Only three things truly need to be pinned early.


4.1 Modular architecture


The system must allow the world to be described as interacting modules.


That is a structural constraint, not a theoretical claim.


4.2 Parameter modulation


Modules must be able to modify parameters in other modules.


Without that, cross-scale interaction becomes impossible.


4.3 Temporal evolution


The system must support state change over time.


A simulation engine that cannot express change would be useless.


Everything else should remain open.

7. Your Chemistry Analogy Is Actually Very Good


Your instinct that these kernel primitives are like periodic table elements is quite accurate.


The difference is that right now you do not yet know the full periodic table.


You are still in a stage similar to chemistry around 1800.


Scientists knew some elements existed, but they had not yet organized them into the periodic system.


Your research phase is essentially the attempt to discover the conceptual periodic table of reality.

9. What Should Remain Non-Negotiable


Even if the ontology evolves, the architectural philosophy probably should not change.


The system should still allow:


• dynamic change

• extensibility
• parameter modulation


These are structural requirements, not metaphysical claims.



10. The Key Principle Going Forward


Treat the kernel like a living constitution, not a set of natural laws.


It should evolve through a process like:

→ architecture update


That ensures the software becomes informed by the research, rather than forcing the research to conform to premature assumptions.
