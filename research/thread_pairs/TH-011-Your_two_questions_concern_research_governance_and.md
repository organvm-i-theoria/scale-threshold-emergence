If the software is designed too early:


• ontology mistakes become hard-coded
• module boundaries become arbitrary
• scaling rules break across layers


If research finishes first:


• modules mirror real scientific domains
• primitives align with actual mechanisms
• cross-domain simulation becomes coherent
