# TH-050 — The_next_step_is_to_formalize_the_research_phase_i

## Prompt

## Response

The next step is to formalize the research phase into a repeatable research operating protocol that governs how each Phase-1 lane is studied, documented, validated, and eventually exposed publicly. The structure below treats the research program itself as a system with QA, versioning, and milestone gates so that it can scale toward dissertations, open research streams, and peer engagement without losing rigor.



Phase-1 Research Operating System (ROS)


This framework governs how each subfield in the Phase-1 matrix is investigated.


The protocol has four layers:

Purpose
